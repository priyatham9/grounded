Grounded · open research by Priyatham Chimmani

Safety numbers and safety AI can both be wrong without anyone noticing.

Six open projects measure where. Three test the injury-rate data that benchmarks are built on, and three test the methods and AI answers that sit on top of it. Everything is public: code, data and tests.
