ehs-human-factors-ontology

hfo-crosswalk-and-banding

Expert-coded crosswalk of performance-influencing factors across HFACS, IDHEAS-G, SPAR-H, CREAM and HSE, plus a rule engine that turns analyst-supplied factor levels into an ordinal screening band. Method described in ehs-human-factors-ontology/README.md.

Assumptions

  • Factor levels are supplied by an analyst; the engine cannot check them. ehs-human-factors-ontology/docs/methods-note.mdFactor levels are inputs.** The engine reasons from levels an analyst supplies and cannot check whether those levels are right.
  • The screening bands rest on thresholds the author chose. ehs-human-factors-ontology/docs/methods-note.mdThe screening bands rest on thresholds the author chose.

Limitations and caveats

  • Same-coder limitation: every match strength is one person's judgement, with no second coder. ehs-human-factors-ontology/docs/methods-note.mdEvery match strength, every asserted absence and every PIF-to-function assignment is one person's judgement. No second coder reviewed them.
  • No inter-rater reliability statistic exists. ehs-human-factors-ontology/docs/methods-note.mdBecause there was one coder, there is no agreement statistic to report.
  • The output is an ordinal band, not a probability, and is not calibrated. ehs-human-factors-ontology/README.mdIt is not a human error probability, not a rate, and not calibrated against anything.
  • Factor-to-error-mode links are unvalidated propositions from the literature. ehs-human-factors-ontology/README.mdNo effect size is attached to any of them and none has been tested against outcome data.
  • Domain transfer from nuclear power and aviation is an extension the sources do not license. ehs-human-factors-ontology/README.mdUsing this vocabulary in chemical manufacturing is an extension, not an application.
  • CREAM conditions were transcribed at one remove, from Shirali et al. (2019). ehs-human-factors-ontology/README.mdHollnagel's book was not consulted; the nine CPCs come from Shirali et al. (2019).
